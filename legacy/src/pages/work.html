How I work

Research. Build. Ship.

01

Research the problem

Start from the papers and the data. For STOCK AI that meant benchmarking classical ARIMA against ML and deep-learning models before writing any UI.

02

Build the core

Get the hard part right first, whether that’s a forecasting pipeline, a lending contract or an evidence parser, then wrap it in clean APIs.

03

Ship & polish

Deploy early (Vercel, GCP, testnets), get feedback and keep iterating on the details people notice.
